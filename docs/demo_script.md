# Demo Script

## Problem
Students often generate valuable signals across attendance, LMS usage, academic performance, and placement-readiness activities, but this information is not always consolidated into one human-friendly view.

## Solution
CampusIQ AI consolidates these indicators into a single dashboard and explains the rules behind each score and risk flag, helping mentors and administrators act early and responsibly.

## Data integration
The application accepts CSV uploads, validates required columns, identifies duplicates, and reports invalid or incomplete rows before importing them.

## Dashboard
The dashboard shows KPI cards for total students, average success score, and support needs. Charts update from the underlying dataset and filters.

## Score calculation
The Student Success Score uses a transparent weighted formula with academic, attendance, LMS, placement readiness, skills, and engagement inputs.

## Risk explanations
Academic and placement risk are separated from overall success scoring. Each risk flag is accompanied by the trigger rule and the observed evidence.

## Segmentation
Students are grouped into actionable segments such as strong academics but weak placement readiness, good attendance but low academic performance, and multiple support indicators.

## Recommendations
The platform recommends supportive next steps like mentoring, targeted practice, and mentor review based on the observed data.

## Expected usefulness
This helps administrators see readiness gaps early, reduce manual analysis effort, and support students with evidence-based follow-up.

## Limits and responsible use
This is a prototype built for a 24-hour hackathon. It supports human review and should not be treated as an automated academic or placement decision engine.
