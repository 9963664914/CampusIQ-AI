# Problem Statement

The challenge asks colleges and academic administrators to build a smarter, evidence-driven system that can predict and explain student success trends.

CampusIQ AI addresses the common operational challenge of fragmented student signals across attendance, coursework, LMS engagement, placement readiness, and academic performance. Faculty often have enough data to intervene, but not enough structured visibility to act early and consistently.

## Target users
- Department administrators
- Faculty and mentors
- Placement coordinators
- Academic support teams

## Proposed solution
CampusIQ AI combines structured student records into a single dashboard, computes a transparent Student Success Score, and flags academic and placement support needs using explainable rules rather than opaque models. The app supports CSV uploads, data validation, and a clear explanation of how each risk indicator is determined.

## Prototype scope
This implementation focuses on:
- student data import and integration
- weighted score calculation
- academic and placement risk detection
- segmentation, recommendations, and dashboards
- documentation and demo-ready workflow

## Evaluation criteria alignment
The app is organized around the provided hackathon criteria: data integration, explainability, dashboard intelligence, and a strong presentation-ready workflow.
