About CampusIQ AI

Problem

Many colleges need a clearer view of student progress, risk signals, and support needs. Staff often work across attendance, academic performance, LMS participation, and placement readiness with limited transparency and insufficient evidence for timely action.

Solution

CampusIQ AI brings together student data from academic, engagement, and placement indicators, then explains a transparent weighted score and flag-based risk signals. The platform is designed to help faculty, mentors, and administrators identify support needs early without relying on opaque models.

Target users

  • Department administrators
  • Faculty and mentors
  • Placement coordinators
  • Academic support teams
